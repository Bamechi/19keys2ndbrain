08

Pressure Test

The strongest case against this plan, answered before the market asks.

Big fish before the pond is full

Fifteen $19K buyers assumes a pipeline that does not exist yet. Answer: the calendar and Creator Days are the pipeline, and Zion keeps its monthly promo cadence.

Three focuses is still nine workstreams

Two people. Answer: sequence inside the quarter. September is Peace on the Pond, New York, and Supermind alignment. Everything else waits.

Perks we cannot yet promise

Factory terms unconfirmed, consult scope unwritten, travel-at-cost feels hidden at $19K. Answer: nothing enters a sales call until it is in writing; price positioned with travel explicitly excluded.

Structure can feel like a cage

If the weekly reads as control, it dies in a month. Answer: the parking lot is real, the quarterly promotes one idea by design, Keys keeps final say.

Supermind is the slowest money

$21.5K on 428 sessions is a traffic problem, and premium rebrands narrow before they widen. Answer: ideal budget only with the 90-day contract signed; sessions on the weekly scorecard.

HLC lives on a channel we do not own

Sponsors will ask about owned reach. Answer: sell Season 6 with Zion, the card game, and live events as owned inventory; the podcast feed as the owned number.
